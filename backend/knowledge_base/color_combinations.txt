# Color Theory & Harmonious Color Combinations in Fashion

## The Science and Emotion of Color
Color is the single most impactful visual stimulus in an outfit. Harmonious color palettes create visual balance, convey mood, flatter skin undertones, and make an ensemble appear cohesive.

## Core Color Pairing Formulas
1. **Monochromatic**: Varying shades, tints, and textures of a single color family (e.g., ivory silk top + oat milk wool coat + cream wide-leg trousers).
2. **Analogous**: Colors adjacent on the color wheel (e.g., olive green + mustard yellow + camel; or blush pink + lavender + rose).
3. **Complementary**: Opposites on the color wheel used with careful moderation (e.g., navy blue accented with terracotta rust, or forest green with warm copper gold).
4. **The 60-30-10 Rule**: 60% dominant base neutral (e.g., charcoal, beige, black), 30% secondary coordinating shade (e.g., cream, sage green), and 10% accent pop (e.g., cherry red bag, gold jewelry, or cobalt shoes).

## Definitive Palette Guide by Hue
- **Black**: Pairs exquisitely with crisp white, warm camel/beige, deep denim blue, soft grey, blush pink, metallic gold, and silver.
- **White & Cream**: Matches almost everything. Most elegant with oatmeal, chocolate brown, olive green, slate grey, denim blue, and pastel lilac.
- **Blue & Denim**: Pairs effortlessly with crisp white, rich camel, chocolate brown, bright cherry red, soft peach, and charcoal grey.
- **Pink & Blush**: Complements white, beige, camel, slate grey, rich burgundy, forest green, and classic denim blue.
- **Red & Crimson**: Pairs with black, charcoal, crisp white, deep navy, camel, and warm metallic accents.
- **Green & Olive**: Combines naturally with ivory, cream, mustard yellow, terracotta, tan leather, and crisp white.
- **Purple & Lavender**: Harmonizes with dove grey, soft cream, sage green, metallic silver, and charcoal.
- **Beige & Tan**: Pairs gorgeously with crisp white, black, chocolate brown, sky blue, sage, and warm terracotta.
- **Brown & Chocolate**: Pairs with cream, sky blue, butter yellow, dusty pink, olive green, and gold jewelry.
