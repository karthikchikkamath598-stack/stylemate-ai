# Cold Weather & Winter Layering Styling Guide

## Mastering the Art of Winter Layering
Cold weather fashion thrives on rich tactile textures, dimensional layering, thermal retention, and sculptural outerwear. Proper layering creates warmth without unflattering bulkiness.

## The Three-Layer Rule
1. **Base Layer**: Thermal moisture-wicking top or fine merino wool turtleneck that hugs the body.
2. **Insulating Mid-Layer**: Chunky cable-knit sweater, cashmere cardigan, or fleece pullover.
3. **Outer Protective Shell**: Wool trench coat, oversized tailored wool blazer, puffer jacket, or shearling-lined coat.

## Winter Outfit Formulas
1. **The Editorial Wool Coat**: Fitted black or cream turtleneck knit + tailored wide-leg houndstooth wool trousers + ankle leather Chelsea boots + double-breasted camel or charcoal wool overcoat + leather crossbody + cashmere scarf.
2. **Elevated Casual Winter**: Chunky oversized fisherman sweater + fleece-lined straight denim or leather trousers + platform lug-sole boots + beanie + puffer jacket.
3. **Winter Ethnic Grace**: Pashmina or velvet embroidered kurti suit + heavy wool-blend shawl or Kashmiri jamawar stole + thermal leggings + closed-toe velvet mojaris or ankle boots.

## Winter Footwear & Accessories
- Footwear: Waterproof leather Chelsea boots, combat boots with lug soles, insulated knee-high boots.
- Accessories: Ribbed cashmere beanies, blanket scarves, leather touchscreen gloves.
