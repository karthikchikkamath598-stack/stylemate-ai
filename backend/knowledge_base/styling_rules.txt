# Master Fashion Styling Rules & Guidelines

## Universal Styling Maxims
Great personal style is systematic. Stylists apply proven principles to bring harmony, intention, and balance to any look.

## Core Rules
1. **The Wrong Shoe Theory**: Wearing a shoe that unexpectedly subverts the expected tone of the outfit makes it immediately chic (e.g., pairing a delicate romantic slip dress with chunky sneakers, or pairing casual gym sweatpants with tailored loafers and a blazer).
2. **The Sandwich Rule**: Balance color and visual weight by echoing the top's color in your shoes or accessories (e.g., black cardigan, light wash jeans, black leather ballet flats).
3. **The Rule of Three Elements**: A complete look needs three pieces: base top + bottom + third layering piece (blazer, cardigan, trench coat, or statement scarf/belt).
4. **Volume Counterbalance**: Pair tight with loose, voluminous with structured. Never wear two sloppy, shapeless items simultaneously unless deliberately going for relaxed oversized loungewear with structured accessories.
5. **Color Harmony Limit**: Keep outfits to a maximum of 3 core colors (the 60-30-10 palette distribution) to ensure visual coherence.
6. **Tailoring is Everything**: Even inexpensive garments look couture when hemmed to the proper length and tailored at the shoulders and waist.
