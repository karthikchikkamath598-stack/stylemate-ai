# Traditional Indian & Ethnic Wear Styling Guide

## Heritage Textiles & Graceful Traditional Silhouettes
Traditional styling celebrates timeless drapery, rich artisan weaves, vibrant borders, and cultural heritage across sarees, salwar suits, anarkalis, and lehengas.

## Classic Traditional Ensembles
1. **The Handloom Saree Grace**: Pure Tussar, Chanderi, or Kanjeevaram silk saree + elbow-sleeve tailored blouse with boat neckline + heritage antique gold jewelry + woven embroidered potli bag + comfortable block heels.
2. **Straight Cut Kurta & Cigarette Pants**: Hand-block printed mulmul or chanderi cotton kurta + tailored straight cotton trousers + chiffon or organza dupatta with gota patti border + silver jhumkis + leather juttis.
3. **Flared Festive Anarkali**: Floor-length or calf-length flared anarkali suit in georgette or silk + churidar bottoms + embellished dupatta draped over one shoulder + chandbali earrings + mojari footwear.
4. **Classic Banarasi Lehenga**: Rich brocade silk lehenga skirt + contrasting raw silk blouse + sheer net dupatta with embellished border + polki necklace set + metallic sandals.
