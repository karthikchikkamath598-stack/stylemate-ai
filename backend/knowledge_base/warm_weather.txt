# Warm Weather & Spring/Early Autumn Styling Guide

## Warm Climate Styling Balance
Warm weather (22°C - 28°C) offers the sweet spot in styling: pleasant conditions allowing stylish light layering, diverse textiles, and versatile day-to-night transitions.

## Warm Weather Outfits
1. **Light Layered Urban**: Ribbed sleeveless knit tank + unbuttoned oversized cotton poplin stripe shirt + high-rise straight-leg jeans + sleek court sneakers or flat mules + structured shoulder bag.
2. **Warm Evening Chic**: Bias-cut satin midi skirt + lightweight fine-gauge crewneck t-shirt or cardigan tucked in + block-heel sandals + delicate pendant + mini baguette bag.
3. **Smart Casual Kurta**: Pastel cotton-silk straight kurti with lace hem details + ankle cigarette pants + open-toe kitten heel mules + tote bag.

## Best Fabrics & Hues
- Fabrics: Cotton poplin, lightweight denim, silk-cotton blends, lyocell, bamboo viscose.
- Palette: Soft sage green, warm apricot, lavender, oat milk cream, baby blue, dusty coral.
