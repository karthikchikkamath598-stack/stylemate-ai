# Traditional & Cultural Festival Styling Guide

## Festive Joy, Heritage & Celebration
Festivals like Diwali, Eid, Navratri, Holi, Durga Puja, and Onam unite vibrant heritage textiles, rich colors, intricate hand-embroidery, and joyful silhouettes.

## Festival Ensembles
1. **The Festive Gharara / Sharara Suit**: Richly embellished knee-length kurti + dramatic flared sharara pants + bordered organza or silk dupatta + chandbali earrings + embellished mojaris or block-heel sandals.
2. **Navratri / Dandiya Fusion**: Flared colorful multi-tiered bandhani or mirror-work lehenga skirt + contrasting sleeveless backless blouse + oxidized silver choker, armlets, and bangles + comfortable flat juttis suitable for hours of dancing.
3. **Diwali Silk Saree Splendor**: Deep jewel-toned tissue silk or Chanderi saree with zari border + contrasting elbow-sleeve blouse + layered Kundan or temple jewelry necklace + flowers in hair + potli bag.
4. **Modern Festive Indo-Western**: Draped dhoti pants or pre-stitched saree skirt + hand-embroidered peplum jacket or crop top + contemporary geometric gold earrings + metallic pointed mules.

## Festive Color Palette & Highlights
- Auspicious and joyful tones: Marigold yellow, vermillion red, rani pink, emerald green, royal cobalt, peacock teal, and rich gold accents.
- Fabrics: Chanderi silk, banarasi brocade, georgette, organza, raw silk, and mulmul cotton.
