# Footwear Styling & Shoe Wardrobe Capsule

## Footwear: The Grounding Element of Any Look
A shoe defines the register and context of an entire outfit. The exact same jeans-and-tee combination shifts from sporty with sneakers, to relaxed-chic with loafers, to alluring with strappy block heels.

## Core Shoe Capsule
1. **White Low-Top Leather Sneakers**: The universal shoe. Pairs seamlessly with tailored trousers, slip dresses, denim, skirts, and casual kurtis.
2. **Chunky Lug-Sole Loafers & Mule Loafers**: Adds academic structure to wide-leg trousers, pleated skirts, and casual tailoring.
3. **Pointed-Toe Flats & Slingbacks**: Elongates the leg line while delivering walkable comfort for corporate meetings and upscale dinners.
4. **Strappy Neutral Block Heels (2 to 2.5 inches)**: The workhorse heel for weddings, parties, date nights, and formal events.
5. **Classic Ankle Chelsea Boots**: Cold-weather staple; provides grip, weather resistance, and sleek lines.
6. **Handcrafted Ethnic Juttis & Kolhapuris**: Leather or embroidered juttis ground sarees, kurtas, lehengas, and fusion denim outfits.

## Footwear Proportion Principles
- With wide-leg trousers, choose shoes with a defined toe shape (pointed flat, chisel toe, or chunky sneaker) so trousers do not overwhelm the foot.
- With cropped ankle trousers or midi skirts, exposing the ankle bone creates a slimming visual transition.
