# Textile Science & Fashion Fabrics Guide

## Fabric Properties & Textile Performance
Understanding textile weight, drape, breathability, and tactile texture separates amateur dressing from master styling.

## Natural Fibers
- **Cotton**: Absorbent, hypoallergenic, durable, easy care; essential for everyday tees, denim, shirts, and casual kurtis.
- **Linen**: Made from flax fibers; highest thermal conductivity; creates relaxed luxury textures with natural slubs.
- **Silk**: Lustrous protein fiber; regulates body temperature; drapes luxuriously for evening slips, sarees, and blouses.
- **Wool & Cashmere**: High thermal insulation, moisture repelling; ideal for winter tailoring, coats, and luxury knitwear.

## Synthetics & Blends
- **Lyocell / Tencel**: Sustainable semi-synthetic regenerated wood cellulose; exceptional drape, cool feel, wrinkle-resistant.
- **Modal / Viscose**: Silky touch, breathable, drape-heavy; used for flowy midi dresses and comfort tops.
- **Elastane / Spandex**: Provides necessary 2-5% stretch in denim and tailored pieces for sitting comfort.

## Fabric Pairings for Textural Depth
To avoid a flat, boring outfit, combine contrasting textures:
- Chunky cable-knit wool sweater paired with fluid silk slip skirt.
- Crisp stiff cotton poplin shirt with relaxed supple leather trousers.
- Matte denim paired with a high-shine satin camisole.
