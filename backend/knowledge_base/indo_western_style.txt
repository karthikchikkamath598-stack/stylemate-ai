# Indo-Western Fusion Fashion Styling Guide

## The Modern Fusion Synthesis
Indo-Western fashion blends traditional South Asian craft, drapes, and embellishments with contemporary Western tailoring, silhouettes, and functional ease. It represents modern cosmopolitan identity.

## Signature Indo-Western Formulas
1. **Kurti with High-Waisted Denim**: Short flared or straight A-line block-print cotton kurti + high-waisted mom jeans or straight-leg light-wash denim + clean white sneakers or Kolhapuri mules + silver oxidized statement jhumkas + canvas tote bag.
2. **Crop Top & Dhoti Pants with Cape**: Embellished embroidered crop top + pleated georgette dhoti pants + long sheer flowy floor-length cape + metallic pointed block heels + geometric statement earrings.
3. **Shirt & Brocade Skirt**: Crisp tailored white poplin button-down shirt tucked into a rich flared Banarasi or brocade silk maxi skirt + statement ethnic choker necklace + slicked-back bun + clutch.
4. **Saree with Blazer / Belt**: Pre-draped contemporary saree + structured tailored single-breasted blazer worn over the blouse + sleek metallic belt cinching the waist + pointed heels.
5. **Kurta as a Trench / Overlay**: Long front-slit bandhani or chanderi kurta worn open as a lightweight duster jacket over a plain ribbed tank top and wide-leg trousers + leather loafers.
