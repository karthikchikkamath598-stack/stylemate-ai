# Fashion Accessories: Curation, Jewelry & Bags

## The Power of Accessories
Accessories transform basic clothes into an intentional outfit. They punctuate silhouettes, frame the face, and allow quick mood shifts from day to night.

## Essential Jewelry Frameworks
- **Minimalist Everyday**: Dainty huggie hoops, herringbone flat chain, single solitaire pendant, slender signet ring, slim leather or mesh watch.
- **Statement & Evening**: Sculptural gold or silver drop earrings, chunky chain link necklace, layered cuffs, cocktail rings.
- **Ethnic & Festive**: Oxidized silver jhumkis, kundan or polki chokers, temple gold jewelry, colorful glass bangles, delicate maang tikkas, anklets (payal).

## Handbags by Function and Vibe
- **Tote Bag**: Spacious canvas or structured leather; ideal for college, work, airport travel, and carrying laptops.
- **Crossbody Bag**: Hands-free convenience; ideal for errands, city walking, concerts, and weekend exploring.
- **Shoulder Bag / Baguette**: 90s retro chic; sits snug under the arm; elevates casual denim or slip dresses.
- **Clutch / Minaudiere**: Sleek handheld evening companion for cocktails, black-tie events, and weddings.
- **Potli Bag**: Embroidered drawstring pouch featuring tassels and beadwork; essential for sarees and lehengas.

## Sunglasses, Belts & Hats
- **Belts**: Clean 1-inch leather belt with brushed brass buckle defines the waistline in oversized blazers, knit dresses, and trousers.
- **Eyewear**: Classic cat-eye or rectangular tortoiseshell sunglasses add immediate editorial glamour.
