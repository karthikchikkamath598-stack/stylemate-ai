# Interview & Corporate Professional Styling Guide

## Professional Demeanor & Power Dressing
Job interviews and corporate environments call for crisp tailoring, understated elegance, clean silhouettes, and neutral color cohesion. Your outfit communicates competence, attention to detail, and poise before speaking.

## Professional Interview Formulas
1. **The Modern Power Suit**: Single-breasted tailored blazer with matching straight-leg or cigarette trousers in navy, charcoal grey, black, or beige + silk or poplin ivory blouse tucked in + pointed-toe leather loafers or 2-inch block heels + structured leather top-handle work tote.
2. **Business Smart Separates**: High-waisted wide-leg tailored trousers + fine-knit crewneck sweater or crisp collared button-down + slim leather belt + pointed flats or kitten heels + minimal leather shoulder bag.
3. **Formal Corporate Kurta Ensemble**: Elegant straight-cut chanderi or raw silk kurti in muted pastels, beige, or navy + matching tailored straight pants + minimal cotton-silk dupatta neatly pinned + subtle stud earrings + closed-toe leather mules or flat pumps.
4. **Tailored Sheath Dress**: Knee-length or midi tailored structured shift/sheath dress in olive, navy, or houndstooth + cropped structured blazer + leather pumps + classic leather watch.

## Rules of Professional Interview Attire
- Avoid loud distracting neon colors, jingling jewelry, or overly casual open-toed flip flops.
- Keep jewelry refined: small pearl or metallic studs, simple watch, single delicate pendant.
- Ensure clothing is pristine, wrinkle-free, and well-tailored.
