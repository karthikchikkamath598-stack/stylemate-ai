# Wedding & Grand Occasion Styling Guide

## Grand Wedding & Festive Elegance
Weddings demand opulent craftsmanship, regal fabrics, majestic drapes, and heritage elegance. Guests aim to honor the celebratory nature while maintaining mobility and refined sophistication.

## Wedding Ensembles
1. **The Classic Royal Saree**: Handloom Kanjeevaram silk, Banarasi brocade, or embroidered organza saree in jewel tones (crimson, royal blue, emerald green, warm mustard) + elbow-length embroidered blouse + traditional antique gold temple jewelry + braided hair with fresh jasmine + embroidered juttis or metallic block heels + potli bag.
2. **Contemporary Pastel Lehenga**: Powder pink, dusty mint, or lavender organza lehenga adorned with mirror-work, resham embroidery, or subtle sequin embellishments + sweetheart neckline blouse + delicate net dupatta + uncut Polki or pearl choker necklace + matching maang tikka.
3. **Regal Anarkali Suite**: Floor-length flared georgette or chanderi silk Anarkali gown with intricate zardozi collar embroidery + churidar bottoms + contrast silk dupatta + jhumkas + gilded mojaris.
4. **Western Black-Tie Wedding Guest**: Floor-skimming draped gown or column dress in rich tones (champagne, dusty rose, navy) + delicate diamond or pearl studs + satin evening minaudiere clutch + ankle-strap stilettos.

## Footwear & Accessory Guidelines
- Footwear must prioritize comfort for hours of rituals and dancing: choose block heels, embellished platform wedges, or padded embroidered leather juttis.
- Accessories: Traditional potli bags, raw-silk clutches, kundan/polki necklaces, floral hair accessories.
